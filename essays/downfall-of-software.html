Downfall Of Software

2026-11-05 07:51:18 GMT

While thinking about the title of this article, my perspective on this topic, increasingly zoomed out as I am wont to do. Going from the "downfall of software", to "downfall of engineering", "downfall of capitalism" to the "downfall of civilisation". I suppose this is because problems in scaling systems are fractal and ultimately boils down to the main bottleneck, the human element.

Anywhoo... Why is it that as hardware gets better and software gets worse as time passes. A couple reasons:

  1. Parkinson's Law
  2. Corporate switching from engineering CEOs to sales CEOs
  3. Proprietary hardware design (Lack of ISA standardization)

Future considerations:

  1. Death of Moore's Law

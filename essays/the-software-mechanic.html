The Software Mechanic

Thu, 29 Oct 2026 14:47:35 GMT

In my limited professional experience, it's my belief that the official creation of feature maintenance focused roles, such as a "software mechanic".

Not all software devs I have, I daresay a majority are not qualified to create distributable/ shared code, in light their designs are a net-negative. This currently is a grey area in grading software engineers, when rather this may actually create an opportunity for more of these test and maintenance gifted engineers.

The work of design across industries has always been glorified and therefore those who otherwise may not be otherwise suited will aspire to it. Glorified for good reason though since it is a rare thing, good design. Ironically made even more so by the influx of mechanics.

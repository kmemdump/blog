Thermocline Of Truth

2026-11-06 04:34:46 GMT

A thermocline is the plateau in the change of temperature that occurs in larger bodies of water, such as lakes and oceans.

The thermocline of truth describes the tendency for the middle layers of a hierarchy to present an obfuscation of the truth to the highest layers of leadership. This in my experience occurs as a result of incompetence and self preservation. Occasionally also a foundation for abuse and exploitative behaviour.

The takeaway here is that the highest layers of leadership needs to maintain a direct communication channel with those on the lowest layers of the hierarchy in order to prevent this issue.

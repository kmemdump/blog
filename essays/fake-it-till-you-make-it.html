Fake It Till You Make It

2026-11-06 04:34:46 GMT

"Fake it till you make it" was the anthem of the decade plus period spanning years of artificially depressed interest rates. The growing lack of substance in dominant consumer products such as smart phones and web based services, is the result of this mentality. Credit was easily available, investors were eager to be liberated of their wallets, it was an era of easy money.

Where did this all begin, was the main factor truly the depressed interest rates? No I believe this all began much earlier, in the shift from engineering to sales. Engineering as opposed to sales has an unbounded ceiling, sales however is capped by the total addressable market (TAM) of a given product/service. When revenue growth stalls, the sales machine inevitably turns inward with a cannibalistic bent. That's how the question goes from; "how do we make a better product" to "how do we make more from our product" to "how do we make more?"

The value extractive CEO was the result of this mentality, the real world example that best exemplifies this is the downfall of Boeing.

I've recently binged a bunch on Theranos media, no deep research on my part, just the documentary, the new biopic and the admittedly trashy Amanda Seyfried tv show. So it's no surprise this topic has come to mind again.

No Procedures Communicate

2026-11-06 15:40:37 GMT

I find that in working with other procedure are largely inferior to communication. It has been my experience in software that communication is superior to procedure.

That should be simple enough to understand, however people by and large seem to be predisposed to adding unnecessary process overhead as a rule. Rather than solve the problem, they dance around because creating cruft is easier than communicating effectively.

In the case of the programmer, I find that shared code is the main vein of communication. If learning to communicate effectively was not hard enough already now we have a relatively new skill to master.

And creating procedure around the process is always easier.

So rather creating different version of diagrams and design documents; In the words, one of the better managers I've worked with; "Show me the code"

I dont know how many times I've had the thought, in the time that I've spent writing these design documents we could have simply implemented it and tested it. And eventually I did just did that and continue to do so.
